COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we start.

What kinds of problems can AssetQuant support?+

Business, analytics, reporting, operational, data and technology problems where better evidence and execution can improve an outcome.

Do you only work in real estate?+

No. AssetQuant is industry-agnostic. Real Estate & Investment Analytics is a flagship vertical, while the broader consulting and analytics capability applies across industries.

Is AssetQuant an investment adviser or portfolio manager?+

No. Our financial and investment work is positioned around analytics, reporting, modelling, research and decision-support—not regulated investment-advisory or portfolio-management activity.

Does every engagement use AI?+

No. AI is one capability. We use it only where it adds practical value and can be governed appropriately.
