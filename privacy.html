Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 October 2026

This policy explains how AssetQuant handles personal information submitted through this website. It is intended to provide a clear, proportionate notice for our current website and enquiry workflow.

Information we collect

When you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, work email address, company name and the information you choose to include in your message. We also receive limited technical information ordinarily generated when a website is accessed or a form is transmitted.

Why we use it

We use enquiry information to review and respond to your request, communicate about the matter you contacted us about, protect the website and our services, and maintain appropriate business records. Please do not submit confidential banking credentials, payment-card data, government identifiers, health information or other unnecessary sensitive information through the enquiry form.

Service providers and international processing

The enquiry form is currently transmitted using Formspree, a third-party form-processing service. Formspree may process information submitted through the form and technical information such as IP address, browser/device information and request metadata under its own terms and privacy practices. Processing infrastructure may be located outside India. Website fonts use visitors’ local system fonts, and the Chart.js library used in certain interactive demonstrations is served locally from this website rather than requested from a third-party content-delivery network.

Retention

We retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, related business follow-up, legal obligations, dispute resolution and security. Retention periods may vary depending on the nature of the enquiry.

Your choices and requests

You may ask us to correct or delete personal information you submitted, withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis for processing, or raise a privacy concern. Use the website enquiry form and clearly mark the message as a privacy request. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the current website. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

This business website is intended for professional users and is not directed to children. Please do not submit personal information about a child through the enquiry form.

Indian data-protection framework

AssetQuant intends to operate this website consistently with applicable Indian data-protection requirements, including provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and rules as and when they apply. This policy is not a representation that provisions subject to phased commencement are already in force.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website, our service providers or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will identify the current version.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, use the AssetQuant website enquiry flow and state “Privacy request” at the beginning of your message.
